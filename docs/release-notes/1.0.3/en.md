### New

- **Show a message on the mask.** Turn on **Show a message** in **Settings > Screen Mask** and type a note,
  for example asking people not to shut down the Mac. It appears in large type before the keypad, with an
  **Unlock** button that leads to the keypad. Text and emoji, up to 120 characters and 3 lines.

### Improved

- Digits left half-entered on the keypad are cleared after 30 seconds without input.
- Update notes like these now appear in MaskPad's language.
