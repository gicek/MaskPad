### New

- **Unlock with Touch ID.** On a Mac with Touch ID, rest a finger on the sensor while the keypad shows
  to turn the mask off. It uses the fingerprints that unlock the Mac, and pressing the key still does
  nothing. To turn this off, use **Unlock with Touch ID** in **Settings > Passcode**. If macOS locks
  Touch ID after too many unrecognized fingerprints, Settings says so and lets you enter your Mac
  password to use it again; until then, use the passcode.

### Fixed

- Automatic update checks now run after the mask turns off. Before, a check that came due while the mask
  was on was skipped for a whole day, so a Mac masked at the same time every day never checked.
