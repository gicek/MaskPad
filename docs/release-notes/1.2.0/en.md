### New

- **Now Playing on the mask.** A card in the top-right corner shows the current track or video, with
  artwork, progress and playback controls. Browsers and video players get play/pause only, so the
  mask can't move a window to another video. It is on for new installs; after this update it stays off
  until you turn on **Show now playing** in **Settings > Screen Mask**, because it shows what is playing
  to anyone at the Mac. You can also hide the controls.
- **Redesigned Settings.** Settings now has a sidebar with General, Screen Mask, Unlock & Security,
  Integrations and About, and a preview of the mask that follows your message and media choices.
- **Choose MaskPad's language** in **Settings > General**, independently of the macOS language. It
  switches right away.
- **Resetting the passcode asks first**, and says what changes.

### Fixed

- Opening a second copy of MaskPad, for example from another folder, now shows Settings in the copy
  that is already running instead of starting a second one.
