### New

- **Waits during video and calls.** Turning on when idle now waits while a video, a call, a presentation,
  a game or screen recording keeps the display awake, as the macOS idle lock does. Keep-awake tools such
  as `caffeinate` and Amphetamine don't delay the mask. To turn this off, use **Wait during video and
  calls** in **Settings > Screen Mask**.
- **Offline warning.** A red "Offline" appears in the top-right corner of the mask while the Mac has no
  network connection, so you can see why a task under the mask stopped.
- **Wrong passcode attempts.** The keypad shows how many wrong passcodes were entered since the mask
  turned on, so you know when someone tried.

### Fixed

- **Check for Updates** in the menu is now dimmed while a check is already running.
- Setting a passcode no longer risks losing the previous one if the Keychain refuses the change.
